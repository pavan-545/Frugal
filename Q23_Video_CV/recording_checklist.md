# Q23 Video CV — Recording Checklist & Submission Guide

Use this checklist to ensure your video recording satisfies all compliance and submission requirements before uploading.

---

## 1. Technical & Environment Setup
- [ ] **Duration Check:** Total recorded video length is strictly between **2:00 and 3:00 minutes** (target 2:45).
- [ ] **Audio Quality:** Clear, crisp voice recording free of echo, background noise, or static.
- [ ] **Environment:** Quiet room with zero background disturbance.
- [ ] **Lighting:** Front-facing, well-lit setup ensuring your face is clearly visible.
- [ ] **Camera Framing:** Head-and-shoulders framing with direct eye contact with the lens.
- [ ] **Attire:** Clean, professional appearance.

---

## 2. Content & Topic Compliance
- [ ] **Topic 1 Addressed:** Redefined AI-Native Software Engineering beyond simple code generation.
- [ ] **Topic 2 Addressed:** Discussed a real engineering challenge (Q1 Dynamic Canvas RAF detector & Circuit Breaker).
- [ ] **Topic 3 Addressed:** Detailed responsible AI usage (manual code review, secret isolation, automated testing).
- [ ] **Topic 4 Addressed:** Outlined systematic problem-solving approach without AI tools.
- [ ] **Honesty & Grounding:** Zero fabricated metrics, fake companies, or unverified claims.
- [ ] **Privacy Check:** No confidential API keys, passwords, or personal credentials visible on screen.

---

## 3. Upload & PDF Integration
- [ ] Video uploaded to Google Drive.
- [ ] Share permissions set to **"Anyone with the link can view"**.
- [ ] Shareable Google Drive link copied.
- [ ] **PDF Insertion:** Google Drive Video URL pasted under the **Q23 placeholder** at the end of the unified submission PDF.

---

## 4. Google Drive Submission Link Placeholder

```text
Google Drive Video CV URL: [INSERT GOOGLE DRIVE SHAREABLE LINK HERE]
```
